More than a timer

Most Pomodoro apps only count minutes. A14 Pomodoro is built around the whole study session: plan, focus, recall, reflect and rest.

Three timers

Flow counts up so you can keep going while it's working. Fixed counts down from 15 to 45 minutes or your own length. Adaptive sets its own length from your best recent sessions.

Floating timer

A small panel stays on top of your work. Mark a distraction, switch subject or finish without leaving your window.

Active-recall notes

After each session, type or draw what you remember before checking your notes. Mark weak spots for later review.

Track every subject

Group subjects by category, see time per subject, and watch a study calendar fill up day by day.

Honest study stats

Time, sessions and patterns, with a clear note of how much evidence stands behind each one.

Private by design

No account, no cloud, no ads, no telemetry. Your records stay on your PC, with one-click verified backups.

Two ways to study

Use Quick Mode for a simple timer: pick a subject, press Start. Switch to Full Mode for the complete study cycle with goals, recall, reflection and timed rest. Change any time in Settings.

Built on active recall

Trying to remember is one of the best-supported ways to learn. A14 Pomodoro makes it part of every session.

  • Plan a subject and an optional goal.
  • Focus with the floating timer.
  • Recall the main idea in your own words, typed or drawn.
  • Reflect with a few optional questions.
  • Rest with a break timer that never counts as study.

History, notes and reviews

Every session keeps its goal, recall notes and log. Search your notes, filter by subject or date, and work through your To review list.

Light, dark and your language

Light and dark themes that can follow Windows, adjustable text size and high contrast. Available in English, العربية (right-to-left), Español, Français, Deutsch, Português and 简体中文.

Questions

Is A14 Pomodoro free?

Yes. It's free and open source under GPL-3.0, with no ads or paid tier.

Does it need an internet connection or an account?

No. It works fully offline, and your study records are stored only on your computer.

Why does Windows show a SmartScreen warning?

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows doesn't recognise it. The full source, checksums and matching source package are published with every release so you can check what you install.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

This beta is for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) only.

What is active recall?

Active recall means trying to remember what you studied before looking at your notes. Research shows that retrieving information strengthens memory more than re-reading it.

Can I use it as a plain Pomodoro timer?

Yes. Choose Quick Mode: pick a subject, press Start, and finish when you're done.
