# A14 Pomodoro — Beta 1

**Windows x64 · 0.0.1-beta.1 · Unsigned prerelease**

The first public release of A14 Pomodoro: a study timer with recall notes and a private record of your study. Your records stay on your computer.

## What's in it

- **Three timers.** Flow counts up with no length to pick, so you can keep going while it's working. Fixed counts down from 15, 25, 35, 45 minutes or your own length. Adaptive sets its own length from your recent well-focused sessions, on this subject and across all your subjects.
- **Recall notes and reviews.** After each session, write down what you remember and come back to it later.
- **A floating timer panel** that stays on top while you work, plus Quick Mode for a fast start.
- **History and Stats** built only from your own sessions, with a study calendar and an optional daily study goal.
- **Calm subject colours** shown as a soft line on each subject card, seven languages, and light and dark themes.
- **Local and private:** no account, cloud sync, ads or telemetry. Verified backups in one click.

## Installing

The installer is unsigned, so Windows may show a SmartScreen warning. If you already have A14 Pomodoro installed, back up your records in Settings and quit the app from its tray icon first. Your study data is kept.
